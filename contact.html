Welcome to ZooWana Help

We are here to answer all your questions and guide you through using our platform.

1. How does ZooWana work?

ZooWana is a platform designed to:

  • Facilitate the adoption of shelter animals.
  • Report emergency situations (SOS) involving animals in danger.
  • Provide detailed information on each animal available for adoption.

2. How to adopt an animal?

Here are the steps to adopt an animal through our platform:

  • Browse available animals: Go to the homepage and use the filters (type of animal, gender, etc.).
  • View details: Click on "Details" to learn more about the animal (age, behavior, health, etc.).
  • Fill out the adoption form: Once you've made your choice, click "Adopt" and complete the required information (name, contact, etc.).
  • Confirmation: After submitting your request, our team or the shelter will contact you to finalize the adoption.

3. How to report an emergency (SOS)?

Go to the SOS section. Fill out the form with the necessary information:

  • Animal's location.
  • Type of problem (injured animal, abandoned, etc.).
  • Optionally, add a photo.

Click "Send": We will forward your report to partner organizations or shelters.

4. What types of animals are available for adoption?

Currently, you can adopt:

  • Dogs.
  • Cats.
  • Other animals (depending on availability, such as rabbits, birds).

Use the filters to refine your search.

The adoption conditions

For any adoption, we require supporting documents to complete the adopter's file: an ID card, an original proof of address dated within the last 3 months, and the certificate of commitment and knowledge of the specific needs of the species, completed and signed 7 days before acquiring the animal.
