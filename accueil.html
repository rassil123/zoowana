The right questions to ask before wanting to adopt!

  • ① Am I ready to have a long-term relationship?
  • ② Is everyone in my household in agreement to welcome a new companion?
  • ③ Are any of the people in my household or I allergic?
  • ④ Do I have enough time to take care of it? Does my current lifestyle allow for it?
  • ⑤ Do I have enough space to welcome him? Won’t he take up too much of my living space?

ZooWana – Connecting Pets with Loving Homes

ZooWana is an innovative pet adoption platform designed to connect loving homes with animals in need. Our website offers a seamless and user-friendly experience for adopters, shelters, and pet owners looking to rehome their pets. With ZooWana, users can browse detailed pet profiles, filter by species, breed, age, and location, and contact shelters or owners directly. Our mission is to promote responsible pet adoption and create a compassionate community dedicated to animal welfare. Whether you're looking to adopt or help animals find a new home, ZooWana makes the process easy and transparent.

The adoption conditions

For any adoption, we require supporting documents to complete the adopter's file: an ID card, an original proof of address dated within the last 3 months, and the certificate of commitment and knowledge of the specific needs of the species, completed and signed 7 days before acquiring the animal.
